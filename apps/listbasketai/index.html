ListBasketAI

The shopping list that thinks ahead.

What it does

ListBasketAI is a basket for everything you keep track of: groceries, household, packing, errands. You write it the way you think about it, and AI suggests the items you always forget, tidies the order and keeps your lists reusable.

Features

  • →AI SuggestionsAdds the items you always forget, based on your own lists.
  • →Smart ListsReusable baskets for groceries, trips, moving day, anything.
  • →ScanTurn a receipt or a handwritten note into a clean list.
  • →SearchEvery item you have ever added, one search away.
  • →WidgetsYour list on the home screen, no app opening required.
