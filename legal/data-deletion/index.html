Legal & support

Data Deletion

Last updated: 2026-10-20 · Applies to all SolutionYoursGroup apps

Short answer

Our apps do not use accounts and we do not keep your content on our servers. Deleting the app deletes its data, and you do not need to ask us for anything.

How to delete your data yourself

  1. On-device content (cards, lists, drafts, reports, diary, streak): delete the app, or use the app's own delete/reset option. The data is gone with it.
  2. iCloud copies: iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → select the app → Delete Data.
  3. Analytics and crash reports: these are tied to a device identifier, not to you personally. They are automatically deleted by our providers under their standard retention periods. You can limit them by turning off Analytics & Improvements in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.
  4. Subscriptions: cancelling is separate from deleting data. iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

If you still want to make a request

Email solutionyoursgroup@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request", stating which app and what you would like removed. We will confirm within 30 days. Because we hold no account records, in most cases the answer will be that there is nothing left to delete — and that is the design.
