Philosophy & Principles

Artificial is How It Is Built. Human is Who It Is For.

2026-10-30 · 4 min read · By SolutionYours

Everyone is racing to make machines that think. We are interested in something smaller, harder and fundamentally kinder: tools that help a person think, care, and finish things.

The Principle of Human Intelligence

There is a quiet weariness with modern software. Every app wants to become a social network; every utility wants to harvest contacts, keep you hooked on endless notifications, and squeeze another subscription tier out of an exhausted mind.

"Artificial is the mechanism. Human is the destination. The moment an algorithm serves its own retention metrics rather than the person holding the glass screen, it has lost the plot."

Our Seven Guardrails

  • No dark patterns: Cancel in one tap. No guilt-trip copy. No hidden toggles.
  • On-device first: Your thoughts, worries, pets, and notes belong to you. We do not farm data.
  • Narrow, honest jobs: Do one thing impeccably well, then step out of the way.
  • No notification abuse: You decide when to open the app. We do not beg for your attention.
  • Fast, tactile interfaces: Native Swift and SwiftUI performance with sub-second feedback.

Software crafted with conscience gives you time back. That is what SolutionYours is dedicated to building.
