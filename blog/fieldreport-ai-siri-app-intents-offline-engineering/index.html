FieldReport AI: Hands-Free Inspections with Siri & App Intents

2026-10-28 · 5 min read · By SolutionYours

When you are standing on a wet roof or holding a clipboard in an active construction zone, typing on a touchscreen with gloves is frustrating. Hands-free voice shortcuts change everything.

Native App Intents Architecture

With our latest update to FieldReport AI, field engineers, auditors, and safety inspectors can use Siri and iOS Shortcuts to perform entire workflows hands-free:

  • "Hey Siri, start a new inspection" — Instantly creates an inspection draft with timestamp and location tag.
  • "Hey Siri, open latest inspection" — Resumes the active site walk without navigating multi-level menus.
  • "Hey Siri, check inspection stats" — Provides instant totals of completed, passed, and flagged items.

Offline First Means Zero Downtime

Cell towers drop in basements, steel-frame facilities, and remote job sites. FieldReport AI runs its CoreData and SwiftData store completely local, analyzes photos on-device, and compiles branded executive PDF reports right from the palm of your hand.
